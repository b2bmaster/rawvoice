Your tone, your phrasing, your paragraph rhythm: writing preferences are worth keeping for the next draft.

RawVoice turns those preferences into something you can save.

It works with ChatGPT and Claude. Start with a 15-question Quick interview, or bring a voice profile you already use. You get a detailed profile and a short voice card to review and keep in a private Project.

Then use those files to guide the next post, article or email. If your preferences change, update the profile rather than starting the whole interview again.

The download includes a Project setup for ChatGPT, an installable Claude skill, and a guide to using the same approved voice files in either platform.

AI can still get things wrong. Read the draft, check the facts and make the final call yourself.

RawVoice 1.0 is my first release version. I'd welcome feedback on the interview, the setup and whether the writing feels closer to your own. Keep private profiles and confidential examples out of feedback.
