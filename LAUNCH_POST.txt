I've been working on RawVoice, a Claude skill that turns writing preferences into something you can review and reuse.

You can start with a 15-question Quick interview or bring an existing voice profile. RawVoice creates a detailed profile and a compact voice card. Once you've reviewed them, you can save both in a private Project and use them to guide future drafts.

The idea is to spend less time re-explaining your tone, paragraphing, formatting and dislikes every time you write. The interviewer, writing guidance, editing and optional text cleanup come in one package.

AI can still misunderstand your preferences or add a detail your notes didn't establish. You're responsible for reviewing the draft and checking factual claims before using or publishing it.

I'm interested in feedback on the setup, the usefulness of the interview and whether the resulting writing feels more like you.

The download includes a setup guide, Project instructions and a fictional walkthrough. Keep your personal voice files private; share feedback without confidential examples.
