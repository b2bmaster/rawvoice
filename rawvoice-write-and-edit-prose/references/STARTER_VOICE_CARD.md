# RawVoice — starter voice card

**Status:** UNCALIBRATED. This is a neutral placeholder, not a profile of the user.

## Provisional defaults

- Prefer clear, natural, accurate language.
- Follow the user’s stated tone and audience.
- Preserve the meaning and factual limits of the source.
- Avoid boilerplate, repetitive transitions, made-up anecdotes, invented statistics, and arbitrary line breaks.
- Format content for its publishing destination.

## What is not yet known

- The author’s preferred openings and endings.
- Sentence rhythm, humor, intensity, and vocabulary.
- Strong dislikes, boundaries, and credibility signals.

Run `taste-interviewer` in Quick or Deep mode to replace these generic defaults with the user's confirmed answers.
