RawVoice 1.0
Teach it your voice. Keep the profile.

Make ChatGPT and Claude sound more like you.

Your tone. Your rhythm. The phrases you'd never keep. RawVoice helps turn those preferences into a writing profile you can review, save and use for the next draft.

Start with the optional Taste Interviewer: 15 main questions in Quick mode, one at a time. Or import a profile you already use. RawVoice creates a detailed VOICE_PROFILE.md and a compact VOICE_CARD.md, with confirmed preferences separated from unknowns.

Use the reviewed files in a private ChatGPT or Claude Project. The ChatGPT route uses the supplied Project guide and instructions; Claude also has an installable skill ZIP. You can transfer the same approved profile files between them, without repeating the interview. There is no automatic cross-platform sync.

Shape the writing by audience and format: posts, articles, emails, newsletters or spoken scripts. Update your saved rules as your preferences change. The interviewer, drafting guidance, format editing and optional text cleanup are part of one workflow.

AI can still make mistakes. Review the output and verify factual claims before use or publication. Voice fit depends on your examples, answers, task, model and setup; RawVoice does not promise perfect accuracy or exact imitation.
